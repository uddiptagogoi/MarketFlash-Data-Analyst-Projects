## 🚀 Featured Project: TravelTide Customer Segmentation & Reward Strategy

### Business Context

TravelTide is an e-booking startup that has experienced rapid growth since its launch in 2021. With a growing customer base and extensive travel inventory, the business wanted to better understand its customers and identify which rewards would be most relevant to different customer groups.

### 🎯 Business Objective

The objective of this project was to:

* Identify meaningful customer segments based on travel behavior.
* Analyze customer engagement and booking patterns.
* Create customer-level analytical features using SQL.
* Assign each customer to a behavioral segment.
* Recommend personalized rewards for each segment.
* Translate analytical findings into actionable business recommendations.

---

## 🔎 Analytical Approach

### 1. Data Preparation

Customer and session-level data were filtered and combined to focus the analysis on sufficiently engaged users.

The analysis used relevant information from:

* Customer demographics
* Website sessions
* Flight activity
* Hotel activity
* Booking behavior
* Travel timing
* Group/travel-party behavior

The final dataset was aggregated to the **user level**, creating one analytical record per customer.

### 2. Feature Engineering with SQL

SQL was used to transform raw transactional and behavioral data into customer-level features.

Examples include:

* Session frequency
* Booking activity
* Flight and hotel behavior
* Travel-party characteristics
* Weekend vs. weekday travel behavior
* Family and couple/group indicators
* Customer demographic attributes
* Engagement-related metrics

These features were then used to identify behavioral patterns across customers.

### 3. Customer Segmentation

Customers were classified into meaningful behavioral segments based on their observed travel patterns.

| Segment      | Customers |  Share |
| ------------ | --------: | -----: |
| **Business** |     2,229 | 37.16% |
| **Solo**     |     1,299 | 21.66% |
| **Group**    |       645 | 10.75% |
| **New**      |       555 |  9.25% |
| **Family**   |       419 |  6.98% |

The segmentation shows that **Business and Solo customers form the largest behavioral groups**, making them particularly important audiences for targeted reward strategies.

---

## 💡 Key Business Insight

The analysis supports the hypothesis that TravelTide customers exhibit **distinct travel behaviors** rather than behaving as one homogeneous customer base.

This creates an opportunity to move from a **one-size-fits-all reward strategy** toward **behavior-based personalization**.

### Recommended Strategy

| Customer Segment | Recommended Reward                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------------------------------------- |
| **Business**     | Lounge access / priority travel benefits        |
| **Solo**         | Free checked baggage / flexible travel benefits |
| **Group**        | Group booking discounts                         |
| **New**          | Welcome incentives / first-booking rewards      |
| **Family**       | Family-oriented travel benefits                 |

The goal is to align each reward with the customer's **actual travel behavior and likely preferences**, rather than offering the same incentive to everyone.
